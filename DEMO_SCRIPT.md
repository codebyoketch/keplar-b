# Cairn demo walkthrough

Use the Jezero area shown in the planner. These coordinates are for the local demo grid; the route and every displayed verdict are computed by the app. Placeholder coverage is labeled when live or cached products are unavailable.

1. Open **MISSION INPUTS**. Set the science target to `South rim bedrock` and leave the claim at **Terrain / bedrock**.
2. Leave waypoint drawing on. Click two or three nearby points in the map, beginning near the center and ending farther east. For a short run, use approximately `77.57°E, 18.40°N` and `77.60°E, 18.40°N` (add a third point near `77.62°E, 18.40°N` to lengthen it). After two points, **Calculate mission route** appears below the map.
3. Show the USGS basemap and data footprints. Toggle slope, then switch CTX and HiRISE footprints individually. Point at the map to show the coordinate and covering product readout; the hatch marks areas without footprint coverage.
4. Select **Calculate mission route**. Select each segment in the register and show its product types and the **Needed** observation in the dossier. The weakest segment is marked by its verdict and flags.
5. On that segment, run **STRESS · UNSEEN OBSTACLE**. Show the test result and updated verdict/resource projection. The obstacle case is most illustrative on a segment without high-resolution imagery. Mission verdicts depend on the route, cached/live data and crew assumptions; the interface reports the computed result rather than forcing a GO-to-GO WITH CHANGES transition.
6. Open **VISOR** and choose **SPEAK DIRECTIONS**. The text remains visible if speech synthesis is unavailable. Point out any warning that close-up imagery is missing and boulders are not visible.
7. Open **BRIEF**, then choose **PRINT / SAVE PDF** to export the one-page route brief, including margins, caveats, stress results, sources and the next observation.

If using a prepared fallback demo area, keep the visible **PLACEHOLDER DATA** badge in view and describe evidence as illustrative. Do not present a placeholder footprint or value as a real observation.
